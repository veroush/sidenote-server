It listens for topics you've already written notes on, and quietly shows what you know. Nothing is judged, nothing is recorded.

Not listening

Tap start when you're ready

Running this inside Claude's chat preview can silently block microphone access — you won't get an error, it'll just do nothing. If Start doesn't visibly change to "Listening" within a second or two, open this page directly in your phone's browser instead of the in-chat preview: tap the file card above → the share/external-link icon → open in Chrome. A real browser tab can properly ask you for microphone permission.
Audio is sent from your phone to your laptop over your local wifi network to be transcribed there — it never goes out to the internet. Your laptop's server doesn't save the audio; it transcribes each chunk and discards it. Matching your question to a note happens entirely on your phone. Your notes themselves are saved privately to your account.
